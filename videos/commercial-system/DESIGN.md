# DESIGN.md

## Style Prompt
Bright, premium operational software film for aquaculture. The piece should feel executive, clear, and product-led: more like a polished SaaS keynote than a tutorial or screen recording. Use the existing brand blues and clean-card UI language to present the system as a control room that turns operational complexity into clarity, action, and growth.

## Colors
- `#F6FAFD` primary canvas
- `#FFFFFF` card surfaces
- `#234085` structural brand blue
- `#2AA6D1` signal accent
- `#0F172A` headline/data ink

## Typography
- Headlines: `"Libre Franklin"`
- Data labels and body: `"IBM Plex Sans"`

## Motion Intent
Clean, executive, and fluid. Motion should feel polished and controlled, with clear transitions and no frantic or flashy movement.

## What NOT to Do
- No dark-mode cyberpunk look
- No literal screencast framing with browser chrome
- No purple gradients or neon accents
- No centered generic hero with empty background
- No dense paragraphs or tiny UI text
